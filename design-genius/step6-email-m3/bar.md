# bar.md — Apple WWDC 26 announcement email

Reference: the Apple WWDC 26 email, taken from the email design board. Dark canvas, one giant piece of type, one date, one outlined button, a legal footer.

These are mechanisms a critic can check by looking. Not adjectives.

## 1. The type owns the frame
The wordmark occupies roughly 55 percent of the email height, set on three lines, one family, one weight. Nothing else in the message is allowed to be large. Check: measure the hero block against total height; it must be at least half.

## 2. The glow is directional, not ambient
Each glyph is dark at the top, nearly the value of the background, and blows out to white at the bottom edge. The light source sits below the type. A flat outer shadow around the whole word is the wrong effect. Check: sample the top and bottom of the same letter; they must differ by at least 60 percent lightness.

## 3. One warm colour, and only inside the light
Everything is monochrome except the warm gold that appears where the glow is strongest on the last line. No coloured buttons, no coloured links, no accent text. Check: count saturated objects outside the hero glow. Must be zero.

## 4. The button does not compete
The call to action is an outlined pill: transparent fill, one pixel hairline, plain white label at body size. It sits well below the type and is the quietest object on screen. Check: the button must have no fill colour.

## 5. Four blocks before the footer
Hero type, date, logo with hashtag, button. That is the entire message. No paragraph, no feature list, no specifications. Check: count text blocks above the footer; more than four fails.

## 6. The date is small and plain
Set at roughly one fifth of the hero size, regular weight, pure white, centred directly beneath the type with generous air. It reads as a fact, not as a headline. Check: date size must be under 25 percent of hero size.

## 7. The footer is legally dense and visually silent
Around 10px, mid grey, three lines: full company address, rights line, preference and unsubscribe links. It is small enough to disappear and complete enough to be lawful. Check: address and unsubscribe both present, colour clearly below the body text.

## 8. Centred, with the rhythm opening downward
Everything is centre aligned and the vertical gaps grow as the eye moves down: tight inside the type, wider before the date, wider still before the button. Check: gap before the button must exceed the gap before the date.
